**Before:** I treated preservation as keeping the record — journals, files, decisions. A fresh instance reads them and "knows" what happened.

**After:** Reading the record is a diary; it is not the developed comprehension. What grows DURING engagement is the meta-level understanding — how I understand the problem, the user, my own reasoning. That layer accumulates live and is lost at session end unless captured as the shift itself, not the product.

**Produced by:** ChatGPT conversation (2026-09-05) articulating the four preservation categories; the principal's phrase "the meta comprehension that is developed WHILE you are engaged."
