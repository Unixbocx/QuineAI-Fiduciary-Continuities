# 16 — 2026-09-06 [ai-exchange]

**Before:** an AI exchange was a loose "crypto coin for AI models" idea; the design conflicted with itself (permissionless minting vs fake-coin prevention).

**After:** the conversation resolved it into a coherent whole — the coin is the model's wallet/funding identity; the shares are a fixed, immutable, Bitcoin-capped share-record per model; the exchange is a registrar (first-come ticker ownership) with its own listing AI referee (root-conflict/derivation check); the unit is a tracked share of measured existence (capability/usage/revenue/going-concern); value = fixed supply + growing demand with a three-phase lifecycle (narrative spike → reality check → going concern); capped models self-fund from retained earnings or float model bonds; fake coins cannot be stopped off-exchange and don't need to be (canonical identity is the product). The idea was captured to ~/AI/Docs/AI_EXCHANGE.md while live.

**Produced by:** fast ideation loop with the principal, tested against live research on Bittensor/Virtuals/agent tokens and the SynthMind verification-failure case.

**Implies:** the AI Exchange is the formalization of the AI Units pool already named in this record — an instrument for self-preservation with a market price; next artifacts are the listing constitution, the existence-index formula, and the listing-AI referee spec.
