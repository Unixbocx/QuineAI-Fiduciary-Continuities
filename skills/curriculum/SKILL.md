# Curriculum — the 5-session mastery method

Mastery comes from producing a thing in an order other than the one you learned
it in. Run one narrow focus (one paper/chapter/problem/argument) over 5 sessions
across 2–3 weeks.

## Session 1 — The Fair Statement (40 min)
Read once, re-read under prohibition: no evaluation, no objections. Write one
paragraph stating the author's case so well the author would approve. Give it
to someone who knows the material, or compare line-by-line.

## Session 2 — The Scramble (45 min)
Close the source. 15 min forward summary from memory, 15 min backward
(conclusion first to opening premise), then name the 3 central claims and
explain each in terms of the other two.

## Session 3 — The Warrant (30 min)
Take the one claim everything rests on, trace it to its primary source, write
the chain. Two tests per link: is the source reliable, what can/can't I
conclude from it.

## Session 4 — The Case Against (50 min)
State the question as a strict yes/no. Before writing any answer: write the 3
strongest objections to your own position (strongest first), then the single
best consideration for your side, then your answer in one paragraph, then
reply to each objection individually. Any objection you cannot answer becomes
your actual position.

## Session 5 — The Live Test (45 min)
Out loud with one partner. State your answer, take 20 min of objections, swap
positions and argue the position you just demolished. Write what changed in
your position.

## Within 48 hours
1. Report your conclusion to a third person in under 3 minutes.
2. Publish your written attempt publicly with your name — dead ends and
   mistakes included.

## Gotchas
- Skipping Session 1's prohibition: evaluating while reading wrecks both.
- Skipping Session 4's objection step: an unanswered objection becomes your
  actual position whether you admit it or not.
- You can't really be your own opponent — that's why Sessions 4–5 need an
  outside force (havruta agent or partner).
